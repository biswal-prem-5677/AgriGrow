# AgriGrow India Market, Adoption and Cost Engineering Plan

**Document ID:** AGR-BIZ-026 | **Purpose:** translate national market structure into an affordable, serviceable launch—not a generic subsidy claim.

## 1. Evidence-based segmentation

The official 2015–16 Agriculture Census records 68.45% marginal holdings (<1 ha), 17.62% small holdings (1–2 ha), and an all-holding average of 1.08 ha; the 2020–21 census was still under preparation according to the current census page. This justifies shared/assisted Tier 0–3 products rather than assuming every farm buys a tower. [DES census](https://desagri.gov.in/programs-schemes/agriculture-census-scheme/) · [holding table](https://desagri.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Pocket-2020-Final-web-file.pdf)

NABARD’s NAFIS 2021–22 reports average rural-household monthly income of INR 12,698, which is not a device-budget figure and varies materially by state and household. Affordability decisions therefore use observed willingness-to-pay and seasonal cashflow in each pilot, not a national average. [NABARD](https://www.nabard.org/PressReleases-article.aspx?EID=91&Key=nafis&cid=554&id=25)

| Segment | Primary offer | Ownership / payment | Adoption proof required |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-time / marginal | Tier 0; optional Tier 1 shared | KVK/FPO demonstration, pay-per-test, refundable deposit | Repeat use and correct protocol without sales incentive |
| Small farmer | Tier 1/2 | Own, rent-to-own or FPO pool; visible consumable/service cost | Season-level action/value exceeds ownership and travel cost |
| Horticulture / progressive | Tier 3 and selected Tier 4 | FPO/service-provider lease, campaign fee, annual calibration | Valid campaign outcome, operator utilisation and repair SLA |
| Medium/large | Tier 4/5 | Installed project plus annual O&M | Energy/radio/data availability and water/input outcome evidence |
| KVK/university/government | Tier 3/6/7 | Tender/project agreement and independent validation | Protocol fit, audit/data governance and service capacity |

## 2. Public programmes and channels

PM-KISAN provides income support; it is not a pre-approved device subsidy and AgriGrow must never represent eligibility or funding as guaranteed. Any scheme linkage needs written programme approval, user consent and a separately auditable benefit process. [PM-KISAN](https://pmkisan.gov.in/) states INR 6,000/year in three instalments for eligible landholding farmer families.

The Soil Health Card system is a key partner/constraint: it supports laboratory-tested nutrient status and sample collection, rather than validating instant-probe NPK claims. KVKs have a national technology-assessment/training mandate; FPO policy/guidance under the 10,000 FPO scheme offers an appropriate cooperative channel. [SHC guidance](https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in/files/FAQ_Final_English.pdf) · [ICAR KVK](https://icar.gov.in/en/agricultural-extension-division) · [SFAC FPO](https://sfacindia.com/fpos.aspx/PDFs/EmpanelledCBBO/PDFs/EmpanelledCBBO/Guideliness.aspx)

## 4. Adoption and distribution playbook

Pilot with 3 KVK/FPO partners in different agro-climatic zones; recruit paid field operators; run comparison plots/reference sampling; publish benefits and failures; train local repair/replacement partners; create seasonal consumable kits; measure 90-day activation, valid-measurement rate, action completion, support cost, repair time and re-use. Expand only where `valid measurements/device`, `calibration compliance`, `farmer-reported value`, and `contribution after service` clear pre-registered thresholds.

## 5. Subsidy and financing control

Every subsidy/rental/cooperative offer has a named funder, eligible SKU, farmer payment, title/insurance/loss policy, service term, data-rights statement, termination/transfer path and complaint route. No PM-KISAN/Aadhaar/land record may be used for device eligibility, credit scoring or data sharing without a lawful, documented programme pathway and explicit user notice.
