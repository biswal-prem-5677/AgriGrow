# AgriGrow Patent Opportunities and IP Strategy

**Document ID:** AgriGrow-IP-017 | **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Strategy

Patents should protect genuinely novel technical solutions demonstrated in prototypes, while trade secrets protect calibration datasets, manufacturability parameters and operations where publication would weaken advantage. This is an invention-disclosure backlog, not a legal opinion or proof of patentability. Engage Indian and target-jurisdiction counsel for novelty search, claim drafting, ownership and filing strategy before public disclosure.

## 2. Candidate invention themes

| Theme | Potential technical contribution | Evidence needed before filing |
|---|---|---|
| Context-aware multi-probe measurement | A workflow that binds medium, probe identity, stabilisation and calibration validity to quality-scored field event | Prototype logs showing reduced invalid readings versus fixed-delay/ungated flow |
| Leakage-managed modular electrochemical front end | Swappable probe cartridge with guarded AFE, identity, AC EC measurement and contamination diagnostics | Schematics, measured leakage/noise and reliability data |
| Agricultural uncertainty engine | Advisory confidence calculated from sensor calibration, method, soil/crop applicability and model uncertainty, with safe escalation | Algorithm specification and comparative safety evaluation |
| Offline provenance protocol | Signed/encrypted local event chain that supports correction/supersession and later idempotent sync from shared field devices | Threat model, protocol implementation and power-loss testing |
| Field calibration orchestration | Adaptive calibration scheduling using drift, usage, temperature, reference checks and regional error model | Field evidence that improves validity/cost trade-off |
| Privacy-preserving regional indicators | Aggregate agricultural maps that preserve method/quality provenance while applying disclosure and sampling-bias controls | Formal privacy/utility analysis |

## 3. IP hygiene

Use dated invention disclosures, contributor/assignment records, lab notebooks, source control and design review records. Train teams not to publish conference slides, supplier RFQs, app screenshots or public demos containing an unfiled invention. Contract manufacturers receive only necessary design packages, with access segmentation and return/destruction terms. Verify open-source licences and third-party sensor/reference designs through SBOM and legal review; do not assume hardware purchased from a supplier grants design IP rights.

## 4. Decision framework

File when novelty, detectability of infringement, market relevance, expected life and enforceability justify cost. Prefer defensive publication for useful but non-core improvements where patenting is weak. Conduct freedom-to-operate separately from patentability; an issued patent does not prove the product is free to make or sell. Maintain trade-mark, industrial-design and confidential-know-how tracks alongside utility patent review.
